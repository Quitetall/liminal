# Assurance

## Acceptance Obligations

### OBL-001 — Complete corpus parity is zero on both declared axes

- **scope:** Every pinned corpus document and every predeclared byte/semantic observable under the exact accepted profile.
- **evidence:** Prior observable declaration digest/time ordering, oracle independence audit, exact corpus coverage reconciliation, per-document retained outputs/hashes, zero byte differences, zero semantic differences and reproducible full-corpus replay.

### OBL-002 — Differences and omissions cannot report parity

- **scope:** Seeded byte-only change, seeded semantic change, missing/extra document, unsupported input, corrupted output and profile/pin drift.
- **evidence:** Observed refusal for each predeclared control with exact discrepancy coordinates; checker itself cannot claim parity for empty comparison sets or a silently normalized difference.

### OBL-003 — Independent evidence and scoped decisions control completion

- **scope:** Only this PARITY intervention and its cited source obligations; no broader SAS claim.
- **evidence:** Actual independent verifier findings and their disposition, complete bounded positive/refusal evidence, source-to-evidence reconciliation, and scoped human authorization and deliverable-specific decisions when received. An absent reviewer, unmet dependency or performer assertion cannot satisfy this obligation.

- **scope:** The exact prerequisites and design contract of this bounded intervention.
- **evidence:** The accepted LIM-WAR-0010 profile/corpus manifest and an independently reviewed prior declaration of every byte and semantic observable, retained with its digest before running either compiler comparison. Missing acceptance, unavailable dependency evidence or a changed design blocks the implementation/evidence milestone.

The Fourier transform decomposes a signal
into its constituent frequencies.

The Laplace transform generalizes it
to the complex plane. {#p-laplace}

Every signal admits a spectral decomposition
unique up to a constant phase. {#p-spectral}

Convolution in the time domain corresponds
to multiplication in frequency space.

The sampling theorem bounds the frequencies
a discrete signal can faithfully represent. {#p-sampling}
